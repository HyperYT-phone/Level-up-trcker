Day A · Calisthenics (20–25 min)

  1. Warm-up · 4 min: March in place, arm circles, shoulder rolls, gentle leg swings.
  2. Circuit · 12–15 min: 2 comfortable rounds: 6–10 wall/chair push-ups; 8–12 bodyweight squats; 6–10 glute bridges; 6 bird-dogs per side; 15–20 sec elevated/knee plank.
  3. Cool-down · 4–5 min: Easy walking and gentle stretches; breathe normally.

Day B · Boxing-inspired fitness (15 min, non-contact)

  1. Warm-up · 3 min: Easy marching, shoulder rolls and gentle side steps.
  2. Cardio · 8 min: Alternate 30 sec brisk side steps/marching with 30 sec easy pace; repeat 8 times.
  3. Footwork · 2 min: Slow forward/back steps and side steps in a clear space, moving under control.
  4. Cool-down · 2 min: Slow walk and gentle shoulder/leg stretches.

General fitness only—not fighting instruction. No sparring, contact, hitting people or hard objects, or practicing punches. For boxing classes, use a qualified coach and trusted adult supervision. Stop if pain, dizziness or feeling unwell; keep effort comfortable and take rest days. If tired on Day B, do gentle mobility only.
